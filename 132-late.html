How this works

Your rota

  • Choose your line number at the top. The calendar shows your duty for every day.
  • Tap any day to jump to it. Open the diary book from the strip below to see duty details and write notes.
  • Use the arrows to change month, swipe the calendar, or press ← / → on a keyboard. T jumps to today.
  • Times after midnight (like 00:52) finish the next morning.

Colours

  • Weekday duty, weekday rest day (red edge and label)
  • Saturday, Sunday
  • Today has an amber ring around it.

Year chart

  • Matches your rota: a flat red tint marks rest days, blue marks working days, orange marks holidays.
  • Slide the chart left or right to see the whole year. Use the ‹ › buttons under the chart, or drag with your finger.
  • Because the year chart follows your selected line, changing line number instantly redraws the rest-day pattern.

Picking holidays on the year chart

  • Tap a day to jump to it. Turn on Select days, then tap or drag across days to pick a block (mouse users can just drag).
  • Each picked day turns amber. The bar above the chart shows the count.
  • Tap Mark as holiday to save them all at once, with a single Undo.
  • Tap Clear to empty the selection before confirming.

Holiday planner

  • Or type a From and To date and tap Add holiday. Leave "To" empty for one day.
  • Or open a day in the diary book and use Mark holiday.
  • Removing a holiday can be undone from the toast.

Diary notes

  • Tap a day on the strip to open it as a book page. Write your note on the page — it saves as you type.
  • Days with a note show an amber dot.
  • Notes go with the date, not the line, and stay private on this device.

7-day diary book

  • The strip at the bottom shows your current week. Tap a day to open it as a book page.
  • Swipe left/right or use the arrows to flip through days. The tabs at the top jump to any day in the week.

Year at a glance

  • The small year calendar near the bottom shows every month for your selected line. Tap a day to jump to it.
  • Tap Expand for a larger version with the duty number in each day. R means rest day.
  • The ‹ › buttons change the year.

Backup & restore

  • Export backup saves all your notes and holiday days into one file.
  • Import backup loads a file you exported earlier and adds them back.
  • Notes live only in this browser. Clearing browser data or changing phone deletes them, so export now and then.

Good to know

  • The first week (from Sat 3 Oct 2026) comes straight from the rota sheet.
  • Lines rotate by one each week (251 moves to 252, and 262 back to 251).
  • This app is based on the rota pattern only and is not linked to the company website. Always check official duty information.
